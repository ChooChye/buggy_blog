# Rails Debugging Challenge

You have been given an existing Rails blog application. Several features do not work as expected.

Your task is to investigate the reported problems, identify their root causes, and fix them without rewriting the entire application.

## Reported problems
- The article list is empty even though articles exist.
- Creating an article does not work as expected.
- The app accepts an article without a title.
- Article category information is missing or incorrect.
- Changing an article title unexpectedly changes its URL slug.
- The publication date changes when editing a published article.
- Searching for articles returns unexpected results.
- Opening a nonexistent article produces an error page.
- Deleting a category causes articles to disappear.
- Invalid submissions do not clearly explain what went wrong.

## Expectations
- Fix the root cause of each problem.
- Keep existing functionality working.
- Add or update tests for important fixes.
- Do not rewrite the application from scratch.
- Be prepared to explain the cause of each issue and how you verified the fix.

You may consult official Rails documentation. Complete the work independently.

## Deliverables
1. A working application.
2. A short list of problems found and fixes made.
3. Tests for the important fixes.
4. A brief explanation of your debugging process.
